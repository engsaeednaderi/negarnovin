Negarnovin (نگارنوین) is an Iran-based cloud infrastructure and IT services provider. The company reports that its operations began in 2009. Its published service areas include VPS, dedicated servers, cloud infrastructure, web hosting, domains, SSL certificates and infrastructure support.

The Negarnovin engineering publication shares practical notes and tutorials about Linux administration, KVM and VMware virtualization, networking, server security, storage performance and cloud operations.
