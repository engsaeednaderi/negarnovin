- 99.99% uptime or any SLA percentage

- Claims about being the largest, leading, oldest or most trusted provider

For each numerical claim, retain its definition, measurement window, calculation method, date, scope and supporting record. For data-center coverage, distinguish a provider's physical facilities from reseller locations, upstream providers and service-availability locations.
