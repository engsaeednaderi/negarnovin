## Backlink acceptance rules
1. Prioritize editorially relevant sites with real readers and identifiable editorial standards.
2. Prefer links embedded in genuinely useful technical references, not footer-wide, sitewide or profile-spam links.
3. Use the brand name, plain URL and natural descriptive anchors. Avoid repeating exact-match commercial anchors across every placement.
4. Sponsored and paid links must be disclosed and qualified by the publisher using rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow" as appropriate.
5. Reject automated link packages, mass directory blasts, expired-domain networks, private blog networks, fake reviews and reciprocal-link schemes.
6. Never claim a paid placement is independent journalism.
7. Require a live URL, publication date, author, page title, target URL, link attribute and invoice/contract record for every placement.
8. Check whether the page is indexed and still live before reporting the placement as delivered.

Google identifies buying links for ranking purposes, excessive exchanges, automated link creation and low-value content created to manipulate link signals as link spam. See: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

## Wikipedia source-readiness
The target is not “a certain number of backlinks”. Wikipedia's company-notability guidance asks for significant coverage of the company itself in multiple reliable, independent secondary sources. Routine announcements, press releases, company material and paid/sponsored articles do not establish this requirement.

A source qualifies for the source tracker only if:
- It is independent of Negarnovin.
- It is published by a reliable editorial outlet.
- It discusses Negarnovin itself in meaningful depth.
- It is secondary coverage, not copied PR, an advertisement or a directory entry.
- The article remains accessible and its author/date/publication can be verified.

Before considering an article, collect several substantial independent sources from more than one outlet and assess the actual depth and editorial independence. This is a readiness threshold for review, not a guarantee that Wikipedia will accept an article.

Policy references:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability_(organizations_and_companies)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest

Because the company has an obvious conflict of interest, any Wikipedia participation must be disclosed and handled through the appropriate review process. Never pay anyone who promises to guarantee that the page will remain.
