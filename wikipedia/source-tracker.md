## Source qualification checklist

A candidate source must answer YES to all of the following before it is counted:

1. Is the publisher independent of Negarnovin and its owners?
2. Is the publisher an established editorial outlet with identifiable authors and editorial responsibility?
3. Is the item a secondary source, rather than company material, a product page or a press release?
4. Does it discuss Negarnovin itself in depth, rather than mentioning it in passing?
5. Is the article not paid, sponsored, supplied by the company, or substantially copied from company PR?
6. Is the full article accessible, dated and attributable to a named author or newsroom?
7. Does it remain available at a stable URL?

A technology article that discusses VPS technology generally may be useful for topical authority but does not establish notability for Negarnovin unless it meaningfully discusses the company itself.

## Excluded source types

Do not count the following as independent notability evidence:

- Negarnovin's official website, blog, GitHub repository, social-media accounts or company press kit.
- Company-authored guest posts, profile descriptions and founder interviews written or controlled by the company.
- Paid advertorials and sponsored posts.
- Press releases and syndicated copies of company announcements.
- Hosting directories, review aggregators and vendor profiles.
- Customer reviews or testimonials.
- A link to the company in an article where the company is only incidentally mentioned.

These sources may still be useful for brand consistency, discovery, referral traffic or customer trust. They simply serve a different purpose.
