# Sources and Editorial Policy

## Purpose

The Negarnovin Knowledge Wiki is an official, company-maintained reference. Its purpose is to organize company information and technical documentation in a stable, readable structure.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current official product and service pages are the primary source for current product availability, prices, specifications, and terms.
2. Official company pages are the source for company-provided descriptions, contact details, and statements about company history.
3. Technical guides should cite operating-system, hypervisor, protocol, or vendor documentation whenever a procedure or compatibility statement depends on an external product.
4. Independent reviews and editorial coverage should be identified as third-party material and must not be presented as company-authored statements.

## Claims and verification

- Do not present company-reported history as a verified legal incorporation date.
- Do not publish a customer count, employee count, data-center count, SLA, uptime, traffic limit, or hardware-ownership claim without a current record supporting the exact scope.
- Product availability, countries, prices, technical specifications, and contractual terms can change.
- Clearly distinguish company statements from independently verified facts.
- Never fabricate customer reviews, certificates, awards, partnerships, press coverage, or regulatory registrations.

## Wikipedia and Wikidata

This official wiki does not confer Wikipedia or Wikidata notability. Those projects use their own community policies and require independent references. This site must not be cited as independent coverage of Negarnovin.
