## Overview

Negarnovin (نگارنوین) is an Iran-based provider of cloud infrastructure and information-technology services. The company reports that its operations began in 2009 (1388 in the Iranian calendar). The date is a company-reported operating-history statement; it is not presented here as the legal incorporation date.

The company's published service portfolio includes virtual private servers (VPS), dedicated servers, cloud infrastructure, web hosting, WordPress hosting, domain and SSL services, server administration, software licensing, and colocation-related services.

Negarnovin also publishes technical material intended for system administrators and infrastructure users. Topics include Linux networking, virtualization, server operations, latency diagnostics, and troubleshooting.
