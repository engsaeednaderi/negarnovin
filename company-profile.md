## Business overview

Negarnovin describes its work as providing infrastructure and IT services for individuals, developers, businesses, and organizations. Its published service areas include virtual and dedicated servers, cloud infrastructure, web hosting, domain and SSL services, and related infrastructure support.

Specific product availability, specifications, included resources, network characteristics, and service terms may vary by plan and location. The current product pages and service terms on the official website are the authoritative source for live offers.
