## Choosing infrastructure based on technical requirements

A responsible VPS selection should consider more than headline price or virtual CPU count. Buyers should compare the virtualization model, resource allocation, storage type, network capacity, server location, backup policy, upgrade options, support process and service terms.

NegarNovin recommends defining the workload first—such as website hosting, business applications, development environments, databases or network services—and then matching the published plan specifications to those requirements.
